Privacy

Beta version of this notice, pending legal review.

Who is responsible

Easy Beans Coffee, C. Pizarro 8, 29670 San Pedro de Alcántara, Málaga. Contact: easybeanscafe@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

The NIF or CIF you enter, company details from the Registro Mercantil, your answers to the eligibility questions, your contact details and any documents you upload. We use them to check which grants you can apply for, to prepare and submit applications you ask us to handle, and to email you about your case, deadlines and status updates.

Payments

If you ask us to apply for you, you authorise a card or bank direct debit through Stripe. Stripe stores the payment details; we never see your full card number. We only charge our fee when a grant is paid, and send you an invoice.

Legal basis

Your consent and, when you ask us to apply for you, the steps needed to provide that service.

Who sees it

Only the people handling your case. Data is stored with Supabase in the EU. Emails are sent through Google (Gmail). Payments are processed by Stripe. Company lookups go through our registry data provider. When we apply for you, your details go to the public body running the grant. We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

Enquiries without an application: up to 12 months. Applications and invoices: for as long as grant and tax rules require (usually 5 years), then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your data, or withdraw consent, by emailing us. You can also complain to the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).
